Privacy Policy

Oven2Door respects the privacy of its customers and is committed to protecting their personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through the Oven2Door app and website, how it is used, and how it is protected. The policy should be implemented consistently with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Philippine food-delivery platforms similarly describe the collection and processing of information needed for accounts, orders, payments, and deliveries.

1. Information We May Collect

When using Oven2Door, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Delivery address
  • Account information
  • Order history
  • Payment or transaction information
  • Customer feedback and reviews
  • Device and technical information
  • Information provided when contacting customer support

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your Oven2Door account.
  • Process and confirm orders.
  • Deliver purchased products.
  • Contact customers regarding their orders.
  • Process payments and refunds.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve the app, website, and services.
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

For delivery, only information necessary to complete the order should be shared with the appropriate delivery personnel or service providers. This approach is also reflected in established Philippine delivery platforms' privacy practices.

3. Data Sharing

  • Oven2Door may share necessary information with trusted service providers, such as payment processors, delivery providers, hosting providers, or technical service providers, when required to provide the service.
  • Oven2Door should not sell customers' personal information to third parties for unrelated purposes.

4. Data Security

  • Oven2Door will use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.
  • However, users should also protect their passwords and avoid sharing their account credentials with others.

5. Data Retention

  • Personal information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or fulfill other legitimate purposes.

6. User Rights

  • Subject to applicable Philippine privacy laws, users may have rights regarding their personal information, including the right to request access to or correction of their information and to raise concerns regarding its processing.

7. Cookies and Website Data

  • The Oven2Door website may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, and understand website usage. Users may manage cookie settings through their browser where available.

8. Policy Updates

  • Oven2Door may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any significant changes should be communicated through the website or mobile application.
